MY TAKE · ARCHITECT & FDE GUARDRAILS

四档管线如何选型，哪些边界必须死守

✅ Good fit（首选适用场景）
  • 需要“边说边听、零抢话、抗咳嗽/背景音”的旗舰门店顾问与智能座舱（首选 Tier ① GPT-Live-1）
  • 日呼 10 万+、要求品牌专属高拟人音色（DragonHDFlash MOS 4.34）的客服热线（首选 Tier ③ 混合式）
  • 含大量生僻同音商品名（新茶饮/医药）且追求 $0.10/h 极致成本的场景（首选 Tier ④ 级联式 + Custom Speech）
  • 使用旁路 WSS 慢脑执行算账、查单、联网搜索并通过 SpeechDelivery 受控口播的快慢双脑架构
⛔ Not for（禁用或需切换管线边界）
  • 禁止把 Voice Live API 当作封闭 CCaaS 呼叫中心或单一 LLM 售卖（它是赋能 1000+ 模型的实时语音接口层）
  • 禁止让端到端语音大模型在无旁路校验下直接心算价格、承诺库存或直接修改生产数据库（必须走 committed=false 确认门控）
  • 禁止在阻断 *.cognitive.microsoft.com:443 出向白名单的内网部署 Connected 容器（连续 10 次上报失败将停服）

MY TAKE · ARCHITECT & FDE GUARDRAILS

Where to use GPT-Live-1, and where not to

✅ Good fit
  • Full-duplex flagship store advisors & smart cockpits needing zero self-cutoff on coughs/backchannels (Tier ① GPT-Live-1)
  • High-concurrency contact centers (100k+ calls/day) requiring custom brand voices & DragonHDFlash MOS 4.34 (Tier ③ Hybrid)
  • Domain-heavy retail/medical catalogs with homophones & $0.10/h cost targets (Tier ④ Cascaded + Custom Speech / MAI-Transcribe-2)
  • System One + System Two sidecar WSS architectures separating conversational responsiveness from deterministic tool execution
⛔ Not for
  • Pitching Voice Live API as a closed CCaaS platform or a single LLM (it is a realtime voice interface layer for 1000+ models)
  • Allowing an end-to-end speech model to perform mental arithmetic or mutate production databases without committed=false gating
  • Deploying Connected Speech containers on air-gapped networks blocking *.cognitive.microsoft.com:443 (halts after 10 missed reports)
