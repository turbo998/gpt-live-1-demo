## Public distribution gate

No video is included in Git or cleared merely by this document. A release requires
full visual and complete audio review, metadata/subtitle/attachment inspection,
sanitization of copies only, final decode/playback and hashes of the exact bytes.
Automated OCR, sampled frames, volume checks and successful decoding are insufficient
alone. Do not upload the original customer ZIP or owner-private materials.

Word is rebuilt from reviewed public text and diagrams, not cleaned in place
from the original document. The customer ZIP is rebuilt from a fixed allowlist.
If video review cannot reliably be completed, keep it excluded and the full
media release blocked. A new live recording requires separate authorization and
cost bounds; synthetic answers cannot substitute for real provider responses.
